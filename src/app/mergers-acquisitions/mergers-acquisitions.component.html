Mergers & Acquisitions

The reasons for M&A are diverse. Often, the possibilities for organic growth are limited or even exhausted. This brings inorganic growth through mergers or acquisitions into focus. The positions of buyers and sellers can vary significantly. With our solid practical experience, we are happy to advise and support you in conducting M&A transactions, whether you are on the buyer's or seller's side.

Our services at a glance:

  • Analysis and implementation of M&A transactions
  • Preparation of required contract documentation
  • Share deals and asset deals
  • Management buyouts
  • Shareholder squeeze-out
  • Conducting legal due diligence
  • Advice and support during M&A negotiations
  • Support in (legal) post-merger integration
