Banking, Finance and Capital Markets Law

A well-founded and legally secure financing structure plays a crucial role in the success of companies, whether in the form of capital increases, credit and mezzanine financing, or as part of a crowdfunding project. With our legal and business expertise, we provide you with the necessary support not only in financing matters but also in IPOs and general contract design.

Our services at a glance:

  • Banking contract law
  • Advice and representation of banks and financial service providers vis-à-vis supervisory authorities
  • Support in fulfilling relevant transparency requirements
  • Conducting owner control procedures
  • Consulting on financing matters
  • Representation of minority shareholders (as joint representative) in corporate law review procedures (merger, squeeze-out)
  • Advice on capital market transactions
  • Consulting on capital market compliance issues
  • Advice on capital market disclosure requirements
